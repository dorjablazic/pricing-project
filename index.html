A simple path to professional advice

Three steps. A clearer next step.

01

Start a video consultation

Connect with a licensed veterinarian within 15 minutes, wherever you and your pet feel comfortable.

02

Talk and show your pet

Share what you’ve noticed, ask your questions and show your pet so the veterinarian can visually assess the situation.

03

Know what to do next

Receive advice on the next steps and whether a clinic visit is necessary. Consultation notes can be sent to your regular veterinarian.

Good to know: Medicines cannot be prescribed through a video consultation. For an emergency, contact a veterinary clinic immediately.
